About

I'm a data scientist based in Nairobi, working in Python and SQL, with a background in project management. I build models and the pipelines around them, from cleaning and validating data to deploying something people can actually use.

I currently work at Elink Agencies, where I built forecasting models for rental revenue and occupancy, a classification model to flag late-paying tenants, and automated Excel-to-Python-to-SQL pipelines that replaced manual reporting. Before that, I built churn models and AWS-based ETL pipelines at Phoenix Analytics (cutting processing time by 40%), and an NLP complaint classifier on 500K+ records through ALX Africa.

I hold the AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner credential and completed the ALX Data Science and Machine Learning programme. My degree is a BSc in Project Planning and Management from the University of Eldoret, which helps me turn stakeholder needs into measurable work.

I'm interested in agri-tech, risk and data quality, and I'm open to data science and data engineering roles.
